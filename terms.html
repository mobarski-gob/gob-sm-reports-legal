Terms of Service – GOB SM Reports

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Scope

GOB SM Reports is an internal reporting tool of Go On Board (goonboard.com). It is intended only for authorised Go On Board staff and is not offered to the public.

2. Use

The application reads statistics of the company's own social media accounts, including the Go On Board YouTube channel, with read-only access, and writes them to the company's internal reports. Access is granted by an authorised channel owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. No warranty

The tool is provided "as is" for internal purposes, without warranties of any kind. Figures come from third-party APIs and may be delayed or revised by the platforms.

4. Data
